# Hydraulic Pump Station - Maintenance Manual (demo)

This is sample content for the IoT Sentinel demo. It is not a real manufacturer manual.

## Bearing inspection

Inspect the main shaft bearings every 500 operating hours. Record vibration with the handheld probe at the drive-end and
non-drive-end housings. If vibration stays above 6 mm/s for ten minutes, schedule a bearing replacement within 48 hours.
Use a torque wrench set to 45 Nm when refitting the bearing housing bolts.

## Coolant system

Flush the coolant loop every 1000 operating hours using grade C coolant. Coolant temperature above 70 degrees C for more than
fifteen minutes indicates a blocked heat exchanger or a failing circulation pump; reduce load and inspect the exchanger.

## Pressure faults

Normal discharge pressure is between 4 and 7 bar. A sustained drop below 3 bar suggests a leaking seal or a worn impeller.
Check the shaft seal first. Never open a pressurised line: isolate the pump and bleed the pressure before any work.

## Motor current

Motor current above the nameplate rating for more than five minutes means mechanical overload. Check the coupling alignment and
the bearings before restarting. Repeated overload trips must be reported to the maintenance supervisor.

## Safety

Lock out and tag out the pump before maintenance. Wear gloves and eye protection when handling coolant.
